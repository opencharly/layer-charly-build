# 2026.261.2027 — docs(skill): drop the stale corpus-inconsistency claim from the docs skill

## Summary of changes

Removes a stale "the corpus is broken" claim from the `docs` skill entity's tests section (`charly.yml` → `docs-skill`).

The paragraph said the published marketplace corpus *"is not internally consistent at its current HEAD"* because `recipes/automation/crabbox-deploy.md` references `/charly-tools:crabbox`, *"whose card exists only through the candy-skill complement … — **the marketplace regeneration has not landed the card**"*. That is no longer true: `tools/skills/crabbox/SKILL.md` **exists** in the corpus, and a full generation at the pinned corpus emits **no dangling-reference error**. The paragraph is replaced with a factual statement of why the bare-clone tests use fixtures (zero network fetches; the resolver/download/latestTag seams are injectable), which is the durable reason and does not depend on corpus state.

**Why this matters beyond prose:** the stale claim ships as published site content (`recipes/build/docs.md`) and reads as a known-broken corpus with no owner — exactly the deferred-defect framing the rulebook forbids. The claim was surfaced by `opencharly/docs#127`'s review.

**Closes / relates to:** a follow-up to `opencharly/layer-charly-build#12`; surfaced by `opencharly/docs#127`.

**Breaking change + rollback:** `none` — skill prose. Revert the merge to roll back.
