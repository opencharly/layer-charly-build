## Review round — RCA of the three blocks

All three were correct. They share one root cause in three disguises: **I wrote claims about state without
locating each one at the artefact — and the base — it describes.**

1. **Wrong diffstat (`+8/-8`).** I wrote a line-count instead of parsing `git diff --numstat`, the same
   "parse, do not transcribe" rule I had quoted in the sibling PR's body. Corrected from the measured
   `--numstat`: `+11/-7` for the tree this body now describes. *Missed control:* no measurement step
   between editing and publishing a numeric claim.
2. **Prose asserting a completed retirement.** I checked the sentence against my own branch instead of the
   base the PR merges into. *Missed control:* the claim-vs-artefact check was scoped to the branch, not to
   `origin/main`.
3. **H1 left as `(drained)`.** I updated the intro sentence and the body text but never swept the term
   inside the file I was editing. *Missed control:* the R5 sweep ran on the *path* (`layer-charly-build`)
   but not on the *term being retired* (`drained`), so a same-file occurrence survived.

The root fix for all three is the same shape and is applied here: measure before writing, locate every
state claim at its base, and sweep the term — not just the path — in the same change. Blast radius of the
original: none landed (the PR was blocked before merge); the cost was one review round.
